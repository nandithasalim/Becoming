Rules

  1. Decide before you ask AI. Write 3–5 lines on what you'll build and why, then use AI. AI-allowed interviews grade exactly this.
  2. No-AI drill first, even on bad days. Thirty minutes is enough.
  3. Only Good-odds companies, plus ThreatMark and SerpApi. Nothing else gets deep work.
  4. Post every day: X daily, LinkedIn 3 times a week, Instagram 2 reels a week.
  5. Asleep by 12:30 AM. Twelve hours of work only holds up with real sleep.
